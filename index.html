Manchester City

A page about the best club in the world!

10 Leauge titles. 9 League Cups. 8 FA Cups. 7 Community Shields. 1 Club World Cup. 1 Super Cup. 1 CHAMPIONS LEAGUE. The resume speaks for itself.

Founded in 1894, Manchester City has been an omnipresent junnernaught in the world of British and broadly European Soccer. Wearing their iconic sky blue the "cityzens" have aquired a large, passionate following overtime, especially in the past 15 years.

Fun Facts about Manchester City

  • Man City's all time top goalscorer is Sergio Aguero, with 260 goals.
  • Their biggest rival is Manchester United, and their rivalry is commonly reffered to as the "Manchester Derby"
  • The club is part of the City Football Group, which was acquired by the Abu Dhabi United Group.
